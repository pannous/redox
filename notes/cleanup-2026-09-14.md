## Gotcha found
`bash build_scripts/build-cranelift.sh kernel` fails with
`cd: recipes/core/kernel/source: No such file or directory` — it resolves `cd` relative to
`build_scripts/`. Use the **root** `./build-cranelift.sh` instead. (This contradicts the
older MEMORY.md note; the root script is the working one.)
